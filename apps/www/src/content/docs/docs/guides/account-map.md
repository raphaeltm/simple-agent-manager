---
title: Account Map
description: See your recent projects, chats, tasks, workspaces, machines and ideas on one map, and how they connect.
---

Select **Map** in the main navigation. The Account Map shows your recent projects, chats, tasks, workspaces and machines together, for the projects you own. Projects you're a member of but don't own aren't on it. Ideas with links to chats shown on the map appear as yellow dots. A line connects each idea to its project and to each linked chat. A stronger line marks a chat that is working on the idea.

Use the **Ideas** filter to hide or show ideas and their lines. Select an idea's title to open its idea page. **Active** limits the map to active chats and resources; switch to **All** to include older chats.

The map shows recent work first and loads a limited amount at a time. When it leaves something out, a notice says so, for example how many recent projects and ideas it checked for links, or that some data couldn't be loaded. Open the project or idea page for its full history.
